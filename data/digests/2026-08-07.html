5. Discussion — how do we make the most money (responsibly)?

This is the honest, evolving view. The goal is compounding, not any single trade. Levers we can tune:

  • Regime detection: pause trend-following in choppy/low-volatility markets; our current rules get whipsawed. Add a volatility filter.
  • Options rotary: selling premium (credit spreads / iron condors) in high-IV names can monetize time decay — but only defined-risk.
  • Concentration vs breadth: fewer, higher-conviction names (our 7) vs expanding the universe. Breadth reduces idiosyncratic blowups.
  • Reinvest discipline: profits compound into the book up to a ceiling; we must resist loosening caps after a win streak.
  • Costs & slippage: paper ignores them; live won't. Any live plan must haircut returns for fees.

Bottom line: the engine is a disciplined apprentice, not a Oracle. It will take losers. What compounds is the system — risk-first, evidence-driven, never over-levered. We review, we tighten, we repeat.
